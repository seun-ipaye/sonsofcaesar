# Event address and privacy

## Where is the event?
Some events have a secret address that is only shared with ticket holders. When it's released, it appears on your ticket page and is emailed to ticket holders. The support assistant can't share it, so check your ticket page or your email. Please keep it to ticket holders.

## How my details are used
When you buy tickets we collect your name and email, the names on your tickets, and any email you choose to send a ticket to. We use them to send tickets, check people in at the door, and contact you about the event you bought for, for example if it's moved or cancelled. We don't sell them or add you to marketing lists.

## Payments and email providers
Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see or store your card details. Ticket emails are sent through Resend.

## Removing a photo or deleting my details
To have a gallery photo of you removed, or your details deleted, DM @socfrvr on Instagram.
