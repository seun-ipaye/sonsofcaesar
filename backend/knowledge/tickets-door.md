# At the door

## What to show at the door
Show the QR code for your ticket, on your phone or from the email. Staff scan it at the door. Each QR code works once.

## Everyone in my group has a ticket on my order
Each ticket has its own QR code and its own name. Everyone needs their own code scanned, so the buyer can show them all or send them to friends.

## I can't find my QR code at the door
Give the name on your ticket. Door staff can look up tickets by name and check you in.

## My ticket says used or refunded
A ticket marked used has already been scanned, and each code only works once. A ticket marked refunded is no longer valid. If that looks wrong, speak to door staff or DM @socfrvr.

## Age limit and ID
Events are 18+, and IDs are checked at the door. Bring a valid ID.

## Dress code and last entry
There is no dress code and no last entry time.

## Leaving and coming back
You can leave and come back in as long as you still have your wristband.
