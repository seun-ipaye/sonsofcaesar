# Refunds and cancellations

## Can I get a refund?
All sales are final, so tickets aren't refunded if you can't make it. If the event is cancelled, you get a full refund.

## Combo pass refunds
All sales are final on combo passes too. If one event in a combo is cancelled, you get a refund for that event.

## Asking about a refund
The support assistant can't issue refunds or promise one. For anything about a specific refund, DM @socfrvr on Instagram.
