# Getting your tickets

## How tickets are delivered
After payment, your tickets are emailed to the address you entered at checkout. The email has a QR code for each ticket, a "View tickets" button that opens your order page, and the QR codes attached as images in case your email app blocks pictures.

## I didn't get my tickets
Check your spam or junk folder first. If they aren't there, open your order page and use "didn't get your tickets?" to send them again. If you can't find your order page, DM @socfrvr on Instagram.

## I typed the wrong email
On your order page, use "didn't get your tickets?", enter the correct email and the same tickets are sent there. An order can be re-sent up to 5 times, with about a minute between sends. If you hit the limit, DM @socfrvr.

## My payment went through but my order says it's confirming
Payment confirmation usually takes a moment. If it's taking longer than usual, your tickets will be emailed as soon as it confirms. If nothing arrives, DM @socfrvr.

## My checkout expired
If a checkout expires before you pay, nothing is charged and the tickets are released. You can start a new order if tickets are still available.

## The email never arrives, even after re-sending
Some email providers can delay or reject messages. Your ticket still exists. Give the name on your ticket at the door and staff can look it up, or DM @socfrvr before the event.
