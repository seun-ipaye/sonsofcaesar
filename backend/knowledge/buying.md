# Buying tickets

## How to buy
Pick your tickets on the event's tickets page, enter a name for each ticket, and pay on Stripe's secure checkout page.

## Service fee
A service fee is added at checkout. The checkout button shows the total before you go to payment.

## Ladies and Men pricing
Some ticket tiers have separate Ladies and Men prices. Both options come from the same pool of tickets, so when the tier sells out, both are sold out.

## Tickets for friends
At checkout you can enter a friend's name on their ticket, and optionally their email. If you add an email, your friend is sent their own ticket. You still receive every ticket on the order.

## Combo passes
A combo pass gives you one ticket for each event in the combo. Each event has its own QR code, and each code works once at its own event. A friend on a combo pass gets their tickets for every event together.

## Tickets aren't on sale yet or are sold out
Tickets can't be bought before an event's sales open or after a tier sells out. Follow @socfrvr on Instagram for announcements about upcoming events.

## Transferring a ticket
To transfer a ticket, give the other person your QR code to be scanned at the door. Each code works once, so only the first person to scan it gets in. Only share it with someone you trust, and don't keep a copy for anyone else to use.
